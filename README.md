## About me

I'm Savio. I've been coding since I was 12. Started with random fun projects and hackathon builds, which slowly became my way of learning, building, and understanding the internet business world.

Along the way, I got accepted into [Y Combinator's Spring 2026 batch](https://bookface.ycombinator.com/company/31438), becoming one of the youngest founders from India to get into the program at 18 years and 10 months.

I was also recognized as [Product Hunt's Maker of the Year](https://www.producthunt.com/stories/announcing-the-golden-kitty-awards-2021-winners) in 2021, became an [EVM](https://www.evm.capital/blog/glow-got-acquired.html), and won several hackathons by [Amazon AWS](https://townhall.hashnode.com/aws-amplify-winners), [HarperDB](https://townhall.hashnode.com/harperdb-hashnode-hackathon-winners), [Clerk](https://townhall.hashnode.com/clerkdev-hashnode-hackathon-winners), [Auth0](https://townhall.hashnode.com/auth0-hashnode-hackathon-winners), and others.

Now I'm 19, took the hard decision to skip college, and I'm building [Result](https://result.dev/), my biggest bet so far.

Result is an operating system for the new age of internet businesses. I'm building it for the kind of people I've always looked up to: founders, creators, hackers, and small, high-leverage teams.

Before Result, I built several side projects, including Glow (300,000+ users and #5 in the App Store in under a month) and Iconify (3M+ logo generations).
